#Problem Statement:
Finding the right beauty and makeup products online is often frustrating. Customers get overwhelmed by too many choices, struggle to find items that match their skin tone or type, and often have to switch between multiple tabs just to chat, search, and securely check out.


# Aura-Beauty-AI
A smart, conversational beauty assistant web application. It acts like a personal makeup artist, letting users type what they need in simple natural language and instantly finding the exact product they are looking for from a curated catalog.


#Key Features:

Conversational AI Search: Users can simply type requests (like "I want a matte lipstick" or "Show me a foundation"), and the system instantly matches and recommends the best item.

Smart Preference & Rejection Handling: If a user doesn't like a recommendation, they can click "Not this one," and the app remembers their choice to show a fresh alternative.

Instant Stock Verification: Automatically checks inventory availability in real time so users never try to buy out-of-stock items.

Secure Payment Integration: Features a fully integrated Razorpay Checkout SDK popup, allowing customers to complete simulated secure payments seamlessly within the app.


#Why It's Useful:
Aura Beauty AI bridges the gap between customer discovery and checkout. It saves time, removes decision fatigue by delivering hyper-personalized recommendations, and provides a smooth, professional e-commerce experience from chat to secure payment in one single place
